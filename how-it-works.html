How it works

Free financial calculators, funded by non-intrusive advertising. No sign-up required.

For users

1
Pick a calculator
Choose from compound interest, mortgage repayments, salary breakdowns, and more. Every calculator runs entirely in your browser — nothing is sent to a server.
2
Enter your numbers
Adjust inputs and see results update instantly. Charts, tables, and summary cards give you a full picture at a glance.
3
Make better decisions
Use the insights to compare scenarios, plan contributions, or understand the real cost of a loan. It's your data — we never store it.

For advertisers

AdSums pairs your brand with high-intent financial audiences. Users arrive ready to make decisions about savings, mortgages, and investments — the perfect context for financial services, fintech, and insurance brands.

1
Full-screen, premium placement
Your ad fills the background behind the calculator — no tiny banners or pop-ups. One advertiser per page, maximum impact.
2
Contextual targeting
Choose which calculators to appear on. Mortgage lenders on the mortgage page, ISA providers on compound interest — relevance built in.
3
Simple pricing
Flat monthly rate per calculator page. No bidding, no impression minimums. See our advertise page for details.

Privacy first

All calculations happen client-side in your browser. We don't use cookies, we don't track you across sites, and we don't sell data. The only network requests are for fonts, Chart.js, and the ad creative itself.
