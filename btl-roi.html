Buy-to-Let ROI Calculator

Evaluate your buy-to-let investment with our free ROI calculator. Enter the property value, rental income, and all costs to see your gross yield, net yield, and cash-on-cash return. The detailed breakdown shows exactly where your money goes — from mortgage payments and management fees to maintenance and insurance — so you can make informed investment decisions.

Understanding rental yields

Gross yield is the annual rent divided by the property value, giving a quick comparison metric. Net yield subtracts running costs like maintenance, management fees, and insurance but excludes mortgage payments. Cash-on-cash return measures your annual profit against the actual cash you invested — deposit plus purchase costs — and is the most useful metric for leveraged property investors.

Key costs to include

A realistic BTL assessment must account for void periods when the property sits empty, management fees if you use a letting agent (typically 8-12% of rent), annual maintenance budgets, landlord insurance, ground rent or service charges for leasehold properties, and of course mortgage interest payments. Underestimating these costs is the most common mistake new landlords make.

How to use this calculator

Enter the property value, purchase costs, and deposit. Set your mortgage rate and monthly rent, then add running costs including void periods, maintenance, management fees, and insurance. The calculator shows your annual ROI, monthly cash flow, and multiple yield metrics. Use the breakdown tab to see every income and cost line itemised.
