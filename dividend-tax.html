Dividend Tax Calculator

Work out how much tax you owe on your dividend income with our free UK dividend tax calculator. Updated for 2025/26 rates, it accounts for your other taxable income to determine which tax bands your dividends fall into. Enter your total dividend income and salary to see an instant breakdown of tax owed, effective rate, and net dividends received.

How dividend tax works in the UK

Dividends are taxed at lower rates than employment income. For 2025/26, the rates are 8.75% for basic rate taxpayers, 33.75% for higher rate, and 39.35% for additional rate. Everyone receives a £500 tax-free dividend allowance. Your other income determines which band your dividends are taxed in — dividends sit on top of your salary and other earnings when calculating tax.

Who pays dividend tax

Dividend tax is relevant to company directors paying themselves through dividends, shareholders receiving investment income, and anyone holding shares outside an ISA or pension. Understanding which tax band your dividends fall into helps you plan the most tax-efficient way to extract income from your business or investments.

How to use this calculator

Enter your total annual dividend income and your other taxable income such as salary or pension. The calculator determines how much of each tax band remains available for dividends and shows a clear breakdown of tax owed at each rate, your effective dividend tax rate, and the net amount you receive after tax.
