UK Salary Calculator

Work out your take-home pay with our free UK salary calculator. Updated for the 2025/26 tax year, it calculates income tax, National Insurance contributions, student loan repayments, and pension deductions to show exactly what lands in your bank account each month. Simply enter your gross annual salary to get a full breakdown instantly.

How UK income tax works

The UK uses a progressive income tax system with multiple bands. For 2025/26, the personal allowance is £12,570, followed by the basic rate of 20% up to £50,270, the higher rate of 40% up to £125,140, and the additional rate of 45% beyond that. Your personal allowance tapers by £1 for every £2 earned above £100,000, creating an effective 60% marginal rate in that band.

Understanding your deductions

Beyond income tax, employees pay National Insurance at 8% on earnings between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2% above that. Student loan repayments are calculated on earnings above plan-specific thresholds. This calculator also models salary sacrifice and relief-at-source pension contributions, showing the NI savings from salary sacrifice arrangements.

How to use this calculator

Enter your annual gross salary, select your student loan plan if applicable, and set your pension contribution percentage. Toggle between salary sacrifice and relief at source to compare the impact on your take-home pay. The breakdown tab shows every deduction line by line, while the summary tab gives you annual, monthly, weekly, and daily figures.
