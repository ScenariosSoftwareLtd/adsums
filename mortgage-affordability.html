Mortgage Affordability Calculator

Find out how much you could borrow for a mortgage with our free affordability calculator. Based on your income, deposit, and existing debt commitments, it estimates your maximum borrowing using standard UK lender income multiples. See the property price you could afford and what your estimated monthly payments would be.

How lenders assess affordability

Most UK mortgage lenders use income multiples of 3.5x to 4.5x your gross annual salary to determine maximum borrowing. Joint applicants can combine their incomes. Existing debts such as credit cards, car finance, and personal loans reduce your borrowing power because lenders factor in your total monthly commitments. Lenders also apply a stress test at a higher interest rate to ensure you can still afford payments if rates rise.

Improving your borrowing power

Reducing or clearing existing debts before applying can significantly increase how much you can borrow. A larger deposit lowers your loan-to-value ratio, giving access to better interest rates. Some lenders offer higher income multiples for professionals in certain careers or for applicants with clean credit histories and strong deposit levels.

How to use this calculator

Enter your annual income and optionally a second applicant's income. Add any monthly debt commitments and your deposit amount. Choose between conservative, standard, or maximum income multiples. The calculator shows your estimated maximum borrowing, the property price you could afford, estimated monthly payments, and a stress-tested payment figure.
