Capital Gains Tax Calculator

Calculate your UK Capital Gains Tax liability with our free CGT calculator. Updated for the 2025/26 tax year, it handles both residential property and other assets at the correct rates. Enter your gain, income, and any losses to offset, and see exactly how much CGT you owe with a clear band-by-band breakdown.

How Capital Gains Tax works

CGT is charged on the profit when you sell or dispose of an asset that has increased in value. For 2025/26, the annual exempt amount is £3,000. Residential property gains are taxed at 18% (basic rate) or 24% (higher rate), while other assets are taxed at 10% and 20% respectively. Your income determines how much of the basic rate band is available for gains.

Reducing your CGT bill

You can offset capital losses against gains in the same tax year, or carry forward unused losses from previous years. Using your annual exempt amount each year, transferring assets between spouses, and investing through ISAs are all legitimate ways to reduce your CGT liability. Timing disposals across tax years can also help keep gains within lower rate bands.

How to use this calculator

Enter your total gain from the disposal, select whether it is residential property or another asset type, and input your annual income. Optionally add any capital losses to offset. The calculator applies the annual exempt amount, splits the taxable gain across the correct tax bands, and shows your total CGT liability and effective tax rate.
