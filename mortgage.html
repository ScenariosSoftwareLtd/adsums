Mortgage Calculator

Calculate your monthly mortgage repayments with our free UK mortgage calculator. Enter your property price, deposit, interest rate, and term to see exactly what you will pay each month. The calculator provides a full amortisation schedule, total interest cost, and visual charts showing how your balance reduces over time.

How mortgage repayments are calculated

Repayment mortgages use an amortisation formula that splits each monthly payment between interest and principal. In the early years, most of your payment goes towards interest. Over time, the balance reduces and more of each payment goes to paying off the loan itself. Interest-only mortgages charge only the interest each month, with the full loan amount due at the end of the term.

Choosing the right mortgage term

Shorter mortgage terms mean higher monthly payments but significantly less total interest paid. A 20-year mortgage costs far less in interest than a 30-year term on the same amount. Use this calculator to compare different terms and rates side by side to find the balance between affordable monthly payments and minimising your total cost of borrowing.

How to use this calculator

Enter the property price and your deposit amount or percentage. Set the interest rate and mortgage term in years, then choose between repayment or interest-only. You can also reverse-calculate the interest rate by entering a known monthly payment. Switch between chart, table, and summary views to explore your repayment schedule in detail.
