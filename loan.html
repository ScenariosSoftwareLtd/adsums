Loan Calculator

Work out your loan repayments with our free loan calculator. Enter the amount you want to borrow, the interest rate, and the repayment period to see your monthly payment, total interest cost, and full amortisation schedule. Whether you are comparing personal loans, car finance, or any other borrowing, this tool helps you understand the true cost before you commit.

How loan repayments work

Most personal loans use a fixed repayment structure where you pay the same amount each month. Each payment covers both interest and a portion of the principal. Early in the loan, more of your payment goes to interest. As the balance decreases, more goes towards repaying the principal. Interest-only loans charge just the interest monthly, with the full amount due at the end.

Comparing loan offers

When comparing loans, look at the total cost of borrowing rather than just the monthly payment. A lower rate or shorter term can save you hundreds or thousands in interest. Use this calculator to model different scenarios — try adjusting the rate by a few percentage points or shortening the term to see the impact on your total cost.

How to use this calculator

Enter the loan amount, annual interest rate, and term in years and months. Choose between repayment or interest-only. The calculator shows your monthly payment, total repaid, and total interest with a visual chart and year-by-year amortisation table. Switch between chart, table, and summary views to explore the numbers in the format you prefer.
