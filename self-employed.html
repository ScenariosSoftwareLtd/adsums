Self-Employed Tax Calculator

Calculate your self-employed take-home pay with our free sole trader tax calculator. Updated for the 2025/26 tax year, it works out income tax, Class 2 and Class 4 National Insurance contributions, and student loan repayments on your trading profit. Enter your annual turnover and allowable expenses to see what you actually keep.

How self-employed tax works in the UK

As a sole trader, you pay income tax on your profit — turnover minus allowable business expenses. The tax bands are the same as employed workers: a £12,570 personal allowance, then 20%, 40%, and 45% rates. However, National Insurance works differently. Self-employed individuals pay Class 2 NI at a flat £179.40 per year and Class 4 NI at 6% on profits between £12,570 and £50,270, then 2% above that.

How to use this calculator

Enter your annual turnover and total allowable expenses. The calculator works out your taxable profit and applies the correct tax and NI rates. The breakdown tab shows every deduction from turnover to take-home pay.
