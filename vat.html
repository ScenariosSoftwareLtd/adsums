VAT Calculator

Quickly add or remove VAT with our free UK VAT calculator. Whether you need to work out the VAT-inclusive price of goods, extract the VAT from a gross amount, or calculate VAT at reduced or custom rates, this tool gives you instant results. Enter any amount and see the net, VAT, and gross figures immediately.

Understanding UK VAT rates

The standard UK VAT rate is 20% and applies to most goods and services. A reduced rate of 5% covers items such as home energy, children's car seats, and some energy-saving products. Zero-rated items include most food, children's clothing, and books. Businesses with taxable turnover above £90,000 must register for VAT and charge it on their sales.

Adding vs removing VAT

Adding VAT means multiplying the net amount by the VAT rate and adding it on top. Removing VAT from a gross figure requires dividing by 1 plus the VAT rate — a common mistake is simply subtracting the percentage, which gives an incorrect result. This calculator handles both directions accurately so you always get the right figure.

How to use this calculator

Choose whether you want to add VAT to a net amount or remove VAT from a gross amount. Enter the figure and select or type the VAT rate. The calculator instantly shows all three values: the net amount, the VAT portion, and the gross total. Use the quick presets for standard 20%, reduced 5%, or zero-rated calculations.
