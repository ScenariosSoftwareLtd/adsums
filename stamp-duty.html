Stamp Duty Calculator

Calculate your stamp duty (SDLT) bill with our free UK calculator. Updated for 2025/26 rates, it covers first-time buyers, home movers, additional property purchases, and non-UK resident surcharges. Enter your property price and buyer type to see a band-by-band breakdown of the tax owed, effective rate, and total cost including stamp duty.

How stamp duty works

Stamp Duty Land Tax (SDLT) is a progressive tax paid when you buy a property in England or Northern Ireland. You only pay the rate on the portion of the price within each band, not on the entire amount. For standard purchases from April 2025, there is no tax on the first £125,000, then 2% up to £250,000, 5% up to £925,000, 10% up to £1.5 million, and 12% above that.

First-time buyer relief and surcharges

First-time buyers pay no stamp duty on the first £300,000 of properties costing up to £500,000, and 5% on the portion between £300,000 and £500,000. Additional property buyers pay a 5% surcharge on top of standard rates on every band. Non-UK residents face a further 2% surcharge. These surcharges can add tens of thousands to the purchase cost of higher-value properties.

How to use this calculator

Enter the property price and select your buyer type: first-time buyer, home mover, additional property, or non-UK resident.
